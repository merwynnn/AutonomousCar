## Fifth slice — live algorithm reload

- Added a **Reload algo** toolbar button and `controller.reload` command.
- The backend re-imports `algorithms.debug`, controller modules and finally
  `algorithms.registry`, then rebuilds controller instances. The simulation,
  track, lap timing and vehicle position are preserved; the new controller
  takes over on the next control tick.
- Reload failures are reported to the dashboard and leave the previous working
  controller instances in place.
- Distinguished this from **Reload config**, which reloads YAML and rebuilds
  the MuJoCo world, sensors, estimator and simulation.
- Added a regression test that verifies controller instances are replaced while
  simulation time is preserved.

## Seventh slice — lidar scan motion compensation

- Fixed dashboard lidar placement: scans now carry the world pose of the
  scan-start lidar frame and are rendered there, instead of being re-attached to
  the live car pose at every 30 Hz state update.
- Added `onboard/scan_motion.py`:
  - keeps a short history of estimated `StateEstimate` poses;
  - interpolates the pose at the scan timestamp;
  - transforms points from the scan-start lidar frame to the current lidar
    frame with the estimated relative motion and mount lever arm;
  - uses only estimated odometry, never simulator ground truth.
- `Observation.compensated_lidar` exposes the result; `Observation.lidar_current`
  is the suggested algorithm access path, with a raw fallback.
  `Observation.sensors.lidar` remains raw.
- `Simulation`, `SensorRobot`, the self-test and the mock-board fixture now
  construct the compensator. `OnboardConfig` loads the lidar mount.
- Added dashboard **Compare** layer:
  - red: raw scan interpreted at the current car pose (no compensation);
  - green: odometry-compensated scan at the current car pose;
  - scan-age readout and legend;
  - normal **Lidar** layer still shows the physically correct raw scan at its
    measurement pose.
- `wall_follow` and `gap_follow` now use `observation.lidar_current`; the
  template documents both access paths.
- Added unit tests for forward motion, yaw rotation, mount lever arm, history
  gaps, maximum scan age and `lidar_current` fallback.
